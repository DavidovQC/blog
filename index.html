Here you’ll find details about my various projects, as well as some writeups of mine. I enjoy expounding upon papers that I have read which interest me, going over them at tutorial pace. Part of the motivation for starting this blog in the first place is to make necessary improvements on poly-tikz. Most of my explanations come with a picture, but drawing beautiful TikZ diagrams has always been quite the chore. So I’ve decided to remdy that, by creating a GUI which is easy to use and makes the work go quicker. I also like to write about data structures and algorithms and various books I’ve read.
